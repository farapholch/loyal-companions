Loyal Companions

Eight hand-made dogs for Minecraft Bedrock

Minecraft has one dog. This adds eight, and gives them something to do. They are not reskins: the body of each breed is built from its own measurements, so the dachshund really is low and long and the Saint Bernard really is heavy.

Version __VERSION__ is finished and tested

It goes up on the two places people install Bedrock add-ons from. Nothing to download here yet.

What they do

  • Fetch. Throw the ball — or a stick, or a bone — and your dog runs after it, picks it up and carries it back to your hand. You can see what it is carrying.
  • Take commands. Right-click with a bone to cycle Follow, Stay and Guard. A guard dog goes for monsters that come near you and growls when something is close.
  • Come when called. The dog whistle calls every dog of yours within 96 blocks and puts them back into Follow — the way out of a Stay command you forgot about three valleys ago.
  • Wear collars in eight dye colours, so you can tell your two border collies apart.
  • Have puppies. Feed two tame dogs of the same breed and you get a puppy, born already yours. It grows up.
  • Dig things up. Bones, string, the odd nugget, once in a long while an emerald.
  • Sound like dogs. Pitch follows size: the Pomeranian yaps, the Saint Bernard rumbles.
